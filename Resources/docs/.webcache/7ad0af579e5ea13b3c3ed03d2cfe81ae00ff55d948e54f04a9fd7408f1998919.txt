Gets the [`FieldRepresentingType`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/field/struct.FieldRepresentingType.html "struct std::field::FieldRepresentingType")’s `TypeId` at the given index of the type represented by this `TypeId`.

The more user-friendly version of this intrinsic is [`core::any::TypeId::field`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/any/struct.TypeId.html#method.field "method std::any::TypeId::field").
