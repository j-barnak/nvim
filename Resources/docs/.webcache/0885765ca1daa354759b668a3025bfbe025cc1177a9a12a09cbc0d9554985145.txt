To fully utilize the power of shell scripting, you need to master Regular Expressions. Certain commands and utilities commonly used in scripts, such as [grep](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/textproc.html#GREPREF), [expr](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/moreadv.html#EXPRREF), [sed](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/sedawk.html#SEDREF) and [awk](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/awk.html#AWKREF), interpret and use REs. As of [version 3](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/bashver3.html#BASH3REF), Bash has acquired its own [RE-match operator](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/bashver3.html#REGEXMATCHREF): **=~**.
