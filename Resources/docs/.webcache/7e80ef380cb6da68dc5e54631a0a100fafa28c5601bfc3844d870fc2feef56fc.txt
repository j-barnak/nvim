Linux Boot Protocol is a convention used by the [Bootloaders](https://wiki.osdev.org/Bootloaders "Bootloaders"), such as [GRUB](https://wiki.osdev.org/GRUB "GRUB"), [Limine](https://wiki.osdev.org/Limine "Limine") or basically anything else, and the Linux kernel, similarly to other boot protocols, such as [Multiboot](https://wiki.osdev.org/Multiboot "Multiboot"). Apart from booting the Linux kernel, the protocol might also be of interest for other operating systems because of its ubiquitousness, especially for the embedded devices or less popular architectures (among OS developers); i.e. everything other than x86.
