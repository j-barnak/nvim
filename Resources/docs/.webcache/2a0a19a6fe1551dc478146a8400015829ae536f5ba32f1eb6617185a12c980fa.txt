Dae Dreamer spends his all of his free time reading about operating systems, compilers, languages, IPC, memory management, UI, device management, etc. Even though he spends hours each day reading about OS development and has fleshed his entire OS in his head, Dae claims he never has time to actually work on it - either due to work commitments, a spouse, other hobbies, or just procrastination. But, he considers himself part of the OS development community. Dae is friends with [Mister Perfect](https://wiki.osdev.org/Mister_Perfect "Mister Perfect").
