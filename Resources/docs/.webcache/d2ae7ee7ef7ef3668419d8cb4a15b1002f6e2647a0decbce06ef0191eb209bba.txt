The Low Pin Count bus, (or LPC bus,) is used to connect low bandwidth devices to the CPU such as [serial](https://wiki.osdev.org/Serial_Ports "Serial Ports") & [parallel ports](https://wiki.osdev.org/Parallel_port "Parallel port"), [PS/2 Keyboard](https://wiki.osdev.org/PS/2_Keyboard "PS/2 Keyboard"), PS/2 Mice, [Floppy Disk Controller](https://wiki.osdev.org/Floppy_Disk_Controller "Floppy Disk Controller"), and the [Trusted Platform Module](https://wiki.osdev.org/index.php?title=Trusted_Platform_Module&action=edit&redlink=1 "Trusted Platform Module (page does not exist)")
