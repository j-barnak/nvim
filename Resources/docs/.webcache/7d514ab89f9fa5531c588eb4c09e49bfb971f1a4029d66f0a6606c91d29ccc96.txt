### 25.6 Generating Signals

Besides signals that are generated as a result of a hardware trap or interrupt, your program can explicitly send signals to itself or to another process.

- [Signaling Yourself](https://sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/Signaling-Yourself.html)
- [Signaling Another Process](https://sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/Signaling-Another-Process.html)
- [Permission for using `kill`](https://sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/Permission-for-kill.html)
- [Using `kill` for Communication](https://sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/Kill-Example.html)
