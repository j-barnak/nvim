Rearrange the following equations to get the primes `p,q`  
  
\$N = p \cdot q\$  
\$c_1 = (2 \cdot p + 3 \cdot q)^{e_1} \mod N\$  
\$c_2 = (5 \cdot p + 7 \cdot q)^{e_2} \mod N\$
