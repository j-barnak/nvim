#### 4.6.2. Proofs

Proof objects, that follow a natural deduction style, are available from the Solver interface \[[20](#mourab08b "Leonardo Mendonça de Moura and Nikolaj Bjørner. 
Proofs and refutations, and Z3.")\]. You have to enable proof production at top level in order to retrieve proofs.

```
s.set("produce-proofs", True)
s.add($\varphi$)
assert unsat == s.check()
print(s.proof())
```

The granularity of proof objects is on a best-effort basis. Proofs for the SMT Core, are relatively fined-grained, while proofs for procedures that perform quantifier elimination, for instance QSAT described in Section [6.4](#sec-qsat "6.4. QSAT"), are exposed as big opaque steps.
