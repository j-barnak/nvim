HFS is one of the older filesystems used by Apple for Mac OS, and has since been superseded with [HFS+](https://wiki.osdev.org/HFS+ "HFS+"). You can get the HFS specifications from [Apple](http://developer.apple.com/legacy/mac/library/documentation/mac/Files/Files-99.html)
