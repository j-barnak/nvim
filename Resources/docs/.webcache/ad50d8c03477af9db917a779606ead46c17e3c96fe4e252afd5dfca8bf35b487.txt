Expand description

Equivalent to C’s `ptrdiff_t` type, from `stddef.h` (or `cstddef` for C++).

This type is currently always [`isize`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/primitive.isize.html "primitive isize"), however in the future there may be platforms where this is not the case.
