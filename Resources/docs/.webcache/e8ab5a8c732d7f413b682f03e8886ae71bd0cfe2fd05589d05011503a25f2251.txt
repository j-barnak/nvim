## Safety

The pointer `_to_drop` must be valid for both reads and writes, not only for the duration of this function call, but also until the returned future has completed. See [ptr::drop_in_place](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/ptr/fn.drop_in_place.html "fn std::ptr::drop_in_place") for additional safety concerns.
