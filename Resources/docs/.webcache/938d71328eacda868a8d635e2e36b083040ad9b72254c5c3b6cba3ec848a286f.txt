**Das U-Boot** (or more simply just **U-Boot**) is an open source generic bootloader mainly used in small-footprint systems, such as Chromebooks and single-board computers. Implementations exist for [x86](https://wiki.osdev.org/X86 "X86") PCs and Chromebooks (where it is often the default bootloader) and [PowerPC](https://wiki.osdev.org/PowerPC "PowerPC") Amigas, as well as several [ARM](https://wiki.osdev.org/ARM "ARM"), [MIPS](https://wiki.osdev.org/MIPS "MIPS"), [Blackfin](https://wiki.osdev.org/index.php?title=Blackfin&action=edit&redlink=1 "Blackfin (page does not exist)"), and [RISC-V](https://wiki.osdev.org/RISC-V "RISC-V") SBCs.
