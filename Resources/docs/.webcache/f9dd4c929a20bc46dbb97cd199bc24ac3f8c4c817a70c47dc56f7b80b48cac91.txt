**I²C** (Inter-integrated Circuit) is a serial, half-duplex and low-bandwidth communication bus. Its main use is for interconnecting ICs (EPROMs, microcontrollers) and other low-bandwidth devices. I²C is used for carrying [EDID](https://wiki.osdev.org/EDID "EDID") monitor data through VGA, HDMI and DisplayPort cables using the [DDC](https://wiki.osdev.org/index.php?title=DDC&action=edit&redlink=1 "DDC (page does not exist)") protocol.
