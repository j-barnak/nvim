**MinGW** (Minimalist GNU for Windows) is a port of several GNU utilities to the Windows console environment, including the GCC / binutils toolchain. It is less sophisticated than Cygwin, but more compact with a lower overhead. It is most widely used in conjunction with the [Dev-C++](https://wiki.osdev.org/Dev-C++ "Dev-C++") and CodeBlocks [IDE](https://wiki.osdev.org/Category:IDEs "Category:IDEs").
