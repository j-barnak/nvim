**Open Firmware**, standardized as IEEE 1275, is a system firmware that originated from Sun Microsystems' *OpenBoot*. It is implemented in the Forth programming language and utilizes (and originated) [device trees](https://wiki.osdev.org/Devicetree "Devicetree") for hardware description. Open Firmware is used in Apple [PowerPC-based Macintosh](https://wiki.osdev.org/Power_Macintosh "Power Macintosh") computers (excluding the initial Power Macintosh 6100 series), various IBM and Motorola PowerPC and POWER hardware, SPARC-based Sun Microsystems machines, and the x86-based OLPC project.
