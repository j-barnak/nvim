Every reasonably complete programming language can test for a condition, then act according to the result of the test. Bash has the [test](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/testconstructs.html#TTESTREF) command, various [bracket](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/testconstructs.html#DBLBRACKETS) and [parenthesis](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/testconstructs.html#DBLPARENSTST) operators, and the **if/then** construct.
