Expand description

Equivalent to C’s `signed long long` (`long long`) type.

This type will almost always be [`i64`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/primitive.i64.html "primitive i64"), but may differ on some systems. The C standard technically only requires that this type be a signed integer that is at least 64 bits and at least the size of a [`long`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/os/raw/type.c_long.html "type std::os::raw::c_long"), although in practice, no system would have a `long long` that is not an `i64`, as most systems do not have a standardised [`i128`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/primitive.i128.html "primitive i128") type.
