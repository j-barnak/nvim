The **Libreboot** project provides free, open source (libre) boot firmware based on coreboot, replacing proprietary [BIOS](https://wiki.osdev.org/BIOS "BIOS")/[UEFI](https://wiki.osdev.org/UEFI "UEFI") firmware on specific Intel/AMD x86 and ARM based motherboards.

### A note on AMD support

AMD is not very well supported by Libreboot and [Coreboot](https://wiki.osdev.org/index.php?title=Coreboot&action=edit&redlink=1 "Coreboot (page does not exist)") due to its usage of the propriatary [AGESA](https://wiki.osdev.org/index.php?title=AGESA&action=edit&redlink=1 "AGESA (page does not exist)"), [openSIL](https://wiki.osdev.org/index.php?title=OpenSIL&action=edit&redlink=1 "OpenSIL (page does not exist)") is planned to replace AGESA permanently by the AMD Zen 6 release. OpenSIL is not planned to be officially backported to earlier AMD CPUs, but has been shown to work on systems as old as Zen1.
