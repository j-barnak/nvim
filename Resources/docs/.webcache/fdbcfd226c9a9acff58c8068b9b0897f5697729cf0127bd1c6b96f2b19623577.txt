Let’s also try our original sample program, which was designed to cause extra grief for [the tokenizer](https://beautifulracket.com/basic/the-tokenizer.html) and [the parser](https://beautifulracket.com/basic/the-parser.html):

\#lang basic
30 rem print 'ignored'
35
50 print "never gets here"
40 end
60 print 'three' : print 1.0 + 3
70 goto 11. + 18.5 + .5 rem ignored
10 print "o" ; "n" ; "e"
20 print : goto 60.0 : end

A side effect of making our lines into functions is that they’re available in the DrRacket REPL like any other function. For instance:

(line-10)
one
(line-60)
three
4
(line-70)
uncaught exception: \#\<change-line-signal\>

    1
    2
    3
    4
    5
    6
    7

    > (line-10)
    one

    three
    4
    > (line-70)
    uncaught exception: #<change-line-signal>

![copy to clipboard](https://beautifulracket.com/fonts/feather-v1.1/svg/download.svg)

This last result makes sense: we’re trying to execute a goto outside the context of the program loop.
