                                                      TRANSACTIONS
                               Two-Phase Locking
                               → Strict 2PL: Txn holds X locks until it commits or aborts. May
                                 release S locks earlier, during the shrinking phase.
                               → Strong Strict 2PL: Txn holds all locks (S and X) until it
                                 commits or aborts. Also called "Rigorous 2PL".

                               Deadlock Detection & Prevention

                               → Intention Locks
                               → Understanding performance trade-offs
