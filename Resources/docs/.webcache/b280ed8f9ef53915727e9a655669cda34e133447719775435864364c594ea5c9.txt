We consider a pushdown automaton
as a word-rewriting
system with labelled rules applied only in
a prefix way. The notion of pushdown
transition
graph is then extended to the notion of prefix
transition graph generated by a word-rewriting

and sizes, to ease the construction

we establish the converse

shapes and sizes), produces a PDA whose transition
