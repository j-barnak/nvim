## IRC

There is a well-populated OS Development IRC channel on Libera (irc.libera.chat) at **\#osdev**. Several regular contributors to the OSDev.org wiki and forums are usually present in the channel, and the channel has over twenty years of history from its previous iteration on Freenode - it even predates this wiki by several years!

- Historical logs and channel statistics: [http://osdev-logs.qzx.com](http://osdev-logs.qzx.com)
- Today's log: [https://libera.irclog.whitequark.org/osdev](https://libera.irclog.whitequark.org/osdev)

### IRC client suggestions

Make your own! IRC is one of the simpler protocols to implement, to the point where it could be the first thing you implement after TCP and Telnet. If there's anything tricky about it, it's calculating the maximum length of the actual text, but you can just use a fixed maximum which allows room for all the other fields at their maximum lengths. Or just ignore it, Freenode seems to handle long messages well. In any case, the standard is [RFC 1459](https://tools.ietf.org/html/rfc1459)

Freenode offers an [AJAX IRC web client](http://webchat.freenode.net/) in case you don't have or want to make one.

Other clients:

- [ii](https://tools.suckless.org/ii/) is a file- and FIFO-based IRC client for POSIX systems in under 1000 lines of source code
- [IRSSI](https://irssi.org/) is a popular and powerful text-mode IRC client.
- [Pidgin](https://pidgin.im/) supports a vast [range](https://pidgin.im/plugins/?publisher=all&query=&type=) of different chat services in one client
- [Riot](https://riot.im) has Matrix and IRC in one package
- [sic](https://tools.suckless.org/sic/) is under 250 lines of C for POSIX systems
- [WeeChat](https://weechat.org/) is a somewhat popular rival to IRSSI, but contrary to its name, it has been known to use many times the memory and CPU of IRSSI
- [wircrc](http://felloff.net/usr/cinap_lenrek/wircrc) is a multi-window IRC client in under 300 lines of Plan 9 shell script, which shows what can be done with operating system support
- [xchat](http://xchat.org/) a X11 based IRC chat client, somewhat user friendly and available in many Linux distributions.

See also Wikipedia's [Comparison of Internet Relay Chat clients](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparison_of_Internet_Relay_Chat_clients).

Somewhat related: [Bitlbee](https://www.bitlbee.org/main.php/news.r.html) is an open-source gateway from IRC to many other chat services. It could be a shortcut to using these other services with your OS. It uses the same libpurple as Pidgin, but obviously cannot support images or video.
