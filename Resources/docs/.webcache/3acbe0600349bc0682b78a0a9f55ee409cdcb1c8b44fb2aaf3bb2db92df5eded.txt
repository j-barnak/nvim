## 12 Input/Output on Streams

This chapter describes the functions for creating streams and performing input and output operations on them. As discussed in [Input/Output Overview](https://sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/I_002fO-Overview.html), a stream is a fairly abstract, high-level concept representing a communications channel to a file, device, or process.
