LILO (LInux LOader) was probably the most well known boot loader around, due to its popularity as the [Linux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux) boot loader. It was designed to allow Linux to multiboot and co-exist with other operating systems, such as DOS and Windows.

It was designed, for the most part, to handle Linux and its initrd's, as well as "chain-loading" other boot loaders. To the knowledge of the authors, no "custom" kernel was yet set up to be booted by LILO.

More recently, [GRUB](https://wiki.osdev.org/GRUB "GRUB") started to replace LILO in many Linux distributions because of its higher versatility (and support for custom kernels...).
