# [Advanced Topics](https://dioxuslabs.com/learn/0.7/essentials/advanced/#advanced-topics)

This section covers a variety of advanced topics you may find useful as your dioxus app grows. It covers topic from organizing your app with custom hooks to breaking out of dioxus with effects.

## [Table of Contents](https://dioxuslabs.com/learn/0.7/essentials/advanced/#table-of-contents)

- [Custom hooks](https://dioxuslabs.com/learn/0.7/essentials/advanced/custom_hooks) In addition to the built-in hooks, Dioxus lets you create your own custom hooks to encapsulate logic you can use throughout your components.
- [Component Lifecycle](https://dioxuslabs.com/learn/0.7/essentials/advanced/lifecycle) Every component in dioxus follows the same, mount, diff and drop lifecycle. This section covers how to hook into that lifecycle.
- [Breaking Out](https://dioxuslabs.com/learn/0.7/essentials/advanced/breaking_out) Sometimes the apis provided by dioxus aren't enough. This section covers how to break out of dioxus and use your own apis to manipulate the DOM or call JS functions.
