Expand description

Equivalent to C’s `size_t` type, from `stddef.h` (or `cstddef` for C++).

This type is currently always [`usize`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/primitive.usize.html "primitive usize"), however in the future there may be platforms where this is not the case.
