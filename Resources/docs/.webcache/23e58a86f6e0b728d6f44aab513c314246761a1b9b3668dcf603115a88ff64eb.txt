Expand description

Converts with LLVM’s fptoui/fptosi, which may return undef for values out of range (<https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/10184>)

Stabilized as [`f32::to_int_unchecked`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/primitive.f32.html#method.to_int_unchecked "method f32::to_int_unchecked") and [`f64::to_int_unchecked`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/primitive.f64.html#method.to_int_unchecked "method f64::to_int_unchecked").
