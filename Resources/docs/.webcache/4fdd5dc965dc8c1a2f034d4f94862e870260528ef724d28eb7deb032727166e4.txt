Available on **Unix** only.

Expand description

Unix-specific extensions to [`JoinHandle`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/thread/struct.JoinHandle.html "struct std::thread::JoinHandle").

## Required Methods

1.9.0 · [Source](https://doc.rust-lang.org/src/std/os/unix/thread.rs.html#21)

Extracts the raw pthread_t without taking ownership

1.9.0 · [Source](https://doc.rust-lang.org/src/std/os/unix/thread.rs.html#29)

Consumes the thread, returning the raw pthread_t

This function **transfers ownership** of the underlying pthread_t to the caller. Callers are then the unique owners of the pthread_t and must either detach or join the pthread_t once it’s no longer needed.

## Dyn Compatibility

This trait **is** [dyn compatible](https://doc.rust-lang.org/1.98.1/reference/items/traits.html#dyn-compatibility).

*In older versions of Rust, dyn compatibility was called "object safety".*
