# 38.5. Credits

*Community participation made this project possible.* The author gratefully acknowledges that writing this book would have been unthinkable without help and feedback from all you people out there.

[Philippe Martin](mailto:feloy@free.fr) translated the first version (0.1) of this document into DocBook/SGML. While not on the job at a small French company as a software developer, he enjoys working on GNU/Linux documentation and software, reading literature, playing music, and, for his peace of mind, making merry with friends. You may run across him somewhere in France or in the Basque Country, or you can email him at [feloy@free.fr](mailto:feloy@free.fr).

Philippe Martin also pointed out that positional parameters past \$9 are possible using {bracket} notation. (See [Example 4-5](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/othertypesv.html#EX17)).

[Stéphane Chazelas](mailto:stephane_chazelas@yahoo.fr) sent a long list of corrections, additions, and example scripts. More than a contributor, he had, in effect, for a while taken on the role of *co-editor* for this document. *Merci beaucoup!*

Paulo Marcel Coelho Aragao offered many corrections, both major and minor, and contributed quite a number of helpful suggestions.

I would like to especially thank *Patrick Callahan*, *Mike Novak*, and *Pal Domokos* for catching bugs, pointing out ambiguities, and for suggesting clarifications and changes in the preliminary version (0.1) of this document. Their lively discussion of shell scripting and general documentation issues inspired me to try to make this document more readable.

I'm grateful to Jim Van Zandt for pointing out errors and omissions in version 0.2 of this document. He also contributed an instructive [example script](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/contributed-scripts.html#ZFIFO).

Many thanks to [Jordi Sanfeliu](mailto:mikaku@fiwix.org) for giving permission to use his fine tree script ([Example A-16](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/contributed-scripts.html#TREE)), and to Rick Boivie for revising it.

Likewise, thanks to [Michel Charpentier](mailto:charpov@cs.unh.edu) for permission to use his [dc](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/mathc.html#DCREF) factoring script ([Example 16-52](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/mathc.html#FACTR)).

Kudos to [Noah Friedman](mailto:friedman@prep.ai.mit.edu) for permission to use his string function script ([Example A-18](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/contributed-scripts.html#STRING)).

[Emmanuel Rouat](mailto:emmanuel.rouat@wanadoo.fr) suggested corrections and additions on [command substitution](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/commandsub.html#COMMANDSUBREF), [aliases](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/aliases.html#ALIASREF), and [path management](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/pathmanagement.html). He also contributed a very nice sample `.bashrc` file ([Appendix M](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/sample-bashrc.html)).

[Heiner Steven](mailto:heiner.steven@odn.de) kindly gave permission to use his base conversion script, [Example 16-48](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/mathc.html#BASE). He also made a number of corrections and many helpful suggestions. Special thanks.

Rick Boivie contributed the delightfully recursive *pb.sh* script ([Example 36-11](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/recursionsct.html#PBOOK)), revised the *tree.sh* script ([Example A-16](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/contributed-scripts.html#TREE)), and suggested performance improvements for the *monthlypmt.sh* script ([Example 16-47](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/mathc.html#MONTHLYPMT)).

Florian Wisser enlightened me on some of the fine points of testing strings (see [Example 7-6](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/comparison-ops.html#STRTEST)), and on other matters.

Oleg Philon sent suggestions concerning [cut](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/textproc.html#CUTREF) and [pidof](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/system.html#PIDOFREF).

Michael Zick extended the [empty array](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/arrays.html#EMPTYARRAY) example to demonstrate some surprising array properties. He also contributed the *isspammer* scripts ([Example 16-41](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/communications.html#ISSPAMMER) and [Example A-28](https://tldp.org/LDP/abs/html/contributed-scripts.html#ISSPAMMER2)).

Marc-Jano Knopp sent corrections and clarifications on DOS batch files.
