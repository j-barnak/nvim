### 20.11 Parsing of Numbers

This section describes functions for “reading” integer and floating-point numbers from a string. It may be more convenient in some cases to use `sscanf` or one of the related functions; see [Formatted Input](https://sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/Formatted-Input.html). But often you can make a program more robust by finding the tokens in the string by hand, then converting the numbers one by one.

- [Parsing of Integers](https://sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/Parsing-of-Integers.html)
- [Parsing of Floats](https://sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/Parsing-of-Floats.html)
