SAT in a Nutshell
• Goal:

F = (a + b)(a’ + b’ + c)

• Given a propositional logic (Boolean) formula
• Find a variable assignment such that the formula evaluates to 1
• or prove that no such assignment exists.

• Naïve search algorithm: For n variables, there are 2n
possible truth assignments to be checked.
• First established NP-Complete problem
• But often tractable in practice (millions of variables)
S. A. Cook, The complexity of theorem proving procedures, Proceedings, Third Annual ACM Symp. on the
Theory of Computing,1971, 151-158

DLL versus CDCL
• DLL:
• Search → Hit a dead end → backtrack one step

• CDCL: conflict driven learning and non-chronological backtracking
• Search → hit a dead end → figure out the reason of conflict
• → remember it so avoid hitting it again in the future
• → backtrack to only relevant signals that can resolve the conflict

J. P. Marques-Silva and Karem A. Sakallah, “GRASP: A Search Algorithm for Propositional Satisfiability”, IEEE
Trans. Computers, C-48, 5:506-521, 1999.
