DLL versus CDCL
• DLL:
   • Search → Hit a dead end → backtrack one step



• CDCL: conflict driven learning and non-chronological backtracking
   • Search → hit a dead end → figure out the reason of conflict
   • → remember it so avoid hitting it again in the future
   • → backtrack to only relevant signals that can resolve the conflict


  J. P. Marques-Silva and Karem A. Sakallah, “GRASP: A Search Algorithm for Propositional Satisfiability”, IEEE
  Trans. Computers, C-48, 5:506-521, 1999.
