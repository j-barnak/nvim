Summary
• Spectre exploits the gap between early microarchitectural effects and
late speculation resolution.
• Security is about precise ordering of microarchitectural
• Mitigations encode hardware assumptions; when those assumptions
fail, attacks resurface.
• Performance vs. strict ordering remains the fundamental tension.
