In this tutorial, we made our basic language cooperate better with Racket at large:

-

  We added a def statement for making named [functions](https://beautifulracket.com/basic-3/functions.html).

-

  We added support for [exports](https://beautifulracket.com/basic-3/exports.html) of functions and variables defined in a BASIC program, and [imports](https://beautifulracket.com/basic-3/imports.html) of Racket libraries. We learned about using [parameters](https://beautifulracket.com/appendix/glossary.html#parameter) to exert remote influence on function behavior.

-

  We saw how to adjust [the REPL](https://beautifulracket.com/basic-3/the-repl.html) to make it read & evaluate certain BASIC statements and expressions, by extracting a parser from the middle of our brag grammar.

-

  We made [command-line arguments](https://beautifulracket.com/basic-3/command-line-arguments.html) available to our BASIC programs, and learned how to manage [hygiene](https://beautifulracket.com/appendix/glossary.html#hygiene) correctly when using a macro to introduce identifiers.

I hope you find making programming languages as productive & interesting as I have. Nothing I’ve said here should be taken as definitive. Rather, it’s a starting point for you to develop your own techniques and make your own discoveries. Have fun & break things!
