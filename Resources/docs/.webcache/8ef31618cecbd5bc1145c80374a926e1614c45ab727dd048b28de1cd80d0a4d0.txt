## Overview

Printing is the process of taking text, documents or photos from a computer and putting them onto a paper using a device called printer.

## Printer interface

To print something, you need to establish a connection from the computer to the printer. This can be done through a cable or wireless using Wi-Fi. In the old days, printers used a big port called [parallel port](https://wiki.osdev.org/Parallel_port "Parallel port"). Now most of the printers have a [USB](https://wiki.osdev.org/USB "USB") type-B port.

## Printing basics

The easiest way to print something is using the [parallel port](https://wiki.osdev.org/Parallel_port "Parallel port") because you don't need to have anything else than ports set-up for it to work.
