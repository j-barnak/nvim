🔬This is a nightly-only experimental API. (`c_size_t` [\#88345](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/88345))

Expand description

Equivalent to C’s `ssize_t` (on POSIX) or `SSIZE_T` (on Windows) type.

This type is currently always [`isize`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/primitive.isize.html "primitive isize"), however in the future there may be platforms where this is not the case.
