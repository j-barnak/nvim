Apple's **Power Macintosh** series, which, as its name suggests, use [PowerPC](https://wiki.osdev.org/PowerPC "PowerPC") processors, succeeded the earlier Motorola 680x0 CPU-based Macintosh models in 1994 with the [Power Mac 6100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_Macintosh_6100) series. Most Power Macs used [Open Firmware](https://wiki.osdev.org/Open_Firmware "Open Firmware") for booting, with the exception of the aforementioned 6100 series.
