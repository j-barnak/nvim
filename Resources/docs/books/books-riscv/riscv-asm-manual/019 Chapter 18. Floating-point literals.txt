Chapter 18. Floating-point literals
The assembler supports the same floating-point literal formats as those defined in the C and C++
standards (i.e., decimal floating-point literals with decimal exponents as well as hexadecimal
floating-point literals with binary exponents).

Here are some examples:

 • 3.14159

 • 0.271828e1

 • 0x0.3p-4
