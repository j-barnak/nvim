Scope
This document aims to provide guidance to assembly programmers targeting the standard RISC-V
assembly language, which common open-source assemblers like GNU as and LLVM’s assembler
support. Other assemblers might not support the same directives or pseudoinstructions; their
dialects are outside the scope of this document.
