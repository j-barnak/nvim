                             The MINIX 3 Mascot

    Other operating systems have an animal mascot, so we felt MINIX 3 ought to
have one too. We chose the raccoon because raccoons are small, cute, clever,
agile, eat bugs, and are user-friendly—at least if you keep your garbage can well
locked.
