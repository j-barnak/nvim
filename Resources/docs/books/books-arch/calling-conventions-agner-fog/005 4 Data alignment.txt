Composite objects
Objects of structures and classes are stored by placing the data members consecutively in
memory. Unused bytes may be inserted between elements and after the last element, if
needed, for the sake of alignment. The requirements for alignment are described below.

Additional information for virtual tables and runtime type identification may be added, as
described in chapter 11.
