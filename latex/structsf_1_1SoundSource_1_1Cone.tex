\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Structure defining the properties of a directional cone. 

Sounds will play at gain 1 when the listener is positioned within the inner angle of the cone. Sounds will play at {\ttfamily outer\+Gain} when the listener is positioned outside the outer angle of the cone. The gain declines linearly from 1 to {\ttfamily outer\+Gain} as the listener moves from the inner angle to the outer angle.
